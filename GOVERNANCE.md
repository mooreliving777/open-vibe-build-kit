# Governance

Open Vibe Build Kit is a small community-driven, noncommercial open-source project. Initial maintainer: Leonard E. Moore.

- Anyone may propose improvements via issues and pull requests.
- Maintainers review functionality, tests, accessibility, licensing and alignment with the community mission.
- Maintainers are responsible for accepting contributions, releases, and website deployments.
- Project discussions and decisions should be recorded publicly where reasonable, except private security and conduct reports.
- No paid consulting, customer hosting, affiliate advertising, proprietary product upsells, or subscription programs are operated through this project.

Governance may evolve as contributors join. This is not a representation that independent volunteers already exist.
