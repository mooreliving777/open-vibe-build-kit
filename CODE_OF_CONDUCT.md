# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Open Vibe Build Kit to be a welcoming learning community regardless of age, ability, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, experience level, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, security, and intellectual property.
- Credit contributors and cite source material.
- Ask questions in good faith and welcome different approaches.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, hate speech, sexualized comments or imagery, threats, targeted insults, doxxing, sharing credentials or private information, spam, and persistent disruption are not permitted.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, community events, and public spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately using GitHub's repository **Security → Report a vulnerability** for security-sensitive issues. For conduct reports, contact the repository maintainer privately through an appropriate verified contact channel; **do not publish a sensitive report in an issue**. Until a dedicated confidential conduct-report contact is established, do not submit sensitive identifying details through public channels. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants for serious or repeated violations. Reports will be reviewed fairly, with privacy and safety prioritized where possible.

## Attribution

Adapted in principle from common open-source community conduct guidance, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and [Project Include](https://projectinclude.org/writing_cocs). This is a project-specific policy, not a claim of adopting any external policy verbatim.
